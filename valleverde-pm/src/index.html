Add a calendar

Paste the calendar’s secret address in iCal format. The app reads it to show your meetings; it never changes your calendar.

Where to find it

Google Calendar: on the web, open Settings, choose the calendar under Settings for my calendars, then copy Secret address in iCal format. Company accounts often don’t have it: then choose Import & export, then Export, and import the .zip it downloads with Import a calendar file.

Outlook and Microsoft 365: on the web, open Settings, then Calendar, then Shared calendars. Under Publish a calendar, choose the calendar and Can view all details, choose Publish, then copy the ICS link.

iCloud: in the Calendar app on the web or on a Mac, share the calendar as a public calendar and copy its address. Anyone with that address can see the events.
