# Privacy

Valleverde PM keeps everything on your computer.

- Your data is one file, `valleverde.json`, in the folder shown in Settings. Daily copies go to the `backups` folder next to it.
- The app makes no network requests. Its content-security policy allows none, and it has no accounts, analytics or API keys.
- If you put the data folder in a synced location such as iCloud Drive, that service stores and syncs the file under your own account.
